QUICK GUIDE

Before you contact support

  1. Keep your booking details ready. Have your itinerary, reservation reference and travel dates available.
  2. Explain the problem clearly. State whether you need a change, cancellation, refund, baggage help or another service.
  3. Use official channels when possible. Verify phone numbers, links and support instructions through the provider's official website.
  4. Protect your information. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary payment information with unknown callers.

Can I call the number from a mobile phone?

Do I need my booking number?

It is useful to have your reservation or itinerary information ready. Exact verification requirements can vary by provider and booking type.

Can a refund be guaranteed?

No. Refund eligibility can depend on the airline, hotel, fare, rate and applicable booking terms.

How do I avoid fake support numbers?
