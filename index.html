Frequently Asked Questions

What should I have ready before contacting support?

Keep your itinerary or reservation number, booking email, travel dates, and a clear description of the issue available.

Can I request a refund for a cancelled booking?

Eligibility depends on the reservation's fare, hotel, package, airline, and cancellation terms. Review the booking conditions first.

What if a phone number on a third-party website looks suspicious?

Do not provide passwords, verification codes, or payment details. Verify contact information through Travelocity's official website or your booking confirmation.

Who handles baggage claims?

Depending on the circumstances, baggage claims may need to be handled directly by the operating airline. Check the airline's instructions.

Can I change a flight after booking?

Some reservations can be changed while others have restrictions or fees. Review the fare rules and current booking terms.
